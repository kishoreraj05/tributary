# Commercial use

Tributary is free for non-commercial use under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

## Free, no permission needed

- Students and teachers: coursework, teaching and learning.
- Research: academic or personal research and experiments.
- Personal projects and hobby use.
- Charities, educational institutions, public research bodies and government bodies, as the licence describes them.

## Needs permission

Any use for a business or another commercial purpose, for example:

- Running Tributary to move or process a company's data.
- Offering it, or something built from it, as a product or a hosted service.
- Using it in paid consulting or client work.

For these, you need a separate commercial licence from the copyright holder. To ask for one, contact [@kishoreraj05 on GitHub](https://github.com/kishoreraj05) or open an issue titled "Commercial licence request". Say who you are and how you want to use Tributary.

If you are not sure whether your use counts as commercial, ask first.
